## How to Submit Templates

1. Go to Meta Business Manager
2. Navigate to WhatsApp Manager > Message Templates
3. Click "Create Template"
4. Fill in the details for each template above
5. Submit for review (typically 1-24 hours)

## Template Variables

- {{1}} = Customer name
- {{2}} = Order number / Products / Cart items
- {{3}} = Total amount
- {{4}} = Address / Tracking number / Payment method
- {{5}} = Estimated delivery date

## Notes

- Templates must be in the exact language specified
- Marketing templates have sending limits
- Utility templates can be sent within 24h of last customer message
- All templates require opt-in from customers
